Smart Chatwork

Every related message in one thread, smart @mentions for people and groups, and one-tap message templates — for the Chatwork web app. Free, local-only, trilingual (Tiếng Việt · English · 日本語).

Add to Chrome Privacy Policy

💬 Related messages in one thread

A badge shows how many messages are related; click to read the whole conversation with reply indentation and jump-to-message.

⚡ One-tap template bar

Your frequent messages as chips right above the message box. Pin, drag to reorder, Alt+1…9, per-room favorites.

@ Smart mention

Type @ for a member dropdown with diacritic-insensitive search and automatic romaji for kanji names — 梅津 matches "umezu".

/ Templates & groups

Slash templates with {date} {time} {me} {room} variables, and @group mentions that tag a whole team at once.

Privacy first

No data collection, no analytics, no servers. Everything is stored locally in your browser, and the extension runs only on www.chatwork.com. Details in the privacy policy.
